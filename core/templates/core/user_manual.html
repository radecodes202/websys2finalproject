User Manual

Welcome to the Inventory Management System user manual. This guide provides role-specific instructions to help you navigate and use the system effectively based on your permissions.

Getting Started

The Inventory Management System (IMS) is designed to help small businesses manage their inventory, sales, purchases, and customer relationships.

To begin, log in with your credentials. Your role determines what features you can access:

  • Admin: Full access to all system features including user management
  • Manager: Access to most features including reports, but limited user management
  • Cashier: Focused on sales and customer interactions
  • Inventory Staff: Focused on inventory tracking and management

Dashboard Overview

Upon logging in, you will be redirected to the dashboard. The dashboard provides an overview of key metrics relevant to your role:

  • Total products in inventory
  • Low stock alerts (items below reorder level)
  • Recent sales transactions
  • Pending purchase orders
  • Customer count and recent activity

Use the dashboard to quickly assess the health of your business and take necessary actions based on your role responsibilities.

Common Features

Navigation

The left sidebar contains navigation links based on your role permissions. Use the hamburger menu (☰) on mobile devices or the toggle button on desktop to show/hide the sidebar.

Search Functionality

Throughout the system, you'll find search bars typically located at the top of list views (like Products, Customers, Users, etc.). These search fields allow you to:

  • Filter results in real-time as you type
  • Search by multiple fields depending on the context (name, ID, email, SKU, etc.)
  • Clear the search by clicking the 'x' icon or deleting all text
  • Press Enter to execute the search

Example: In the User Management section, try typing a user's name or email to instantly filter the user list.

Filters and Sorting

Click on column headers in tables to sort data ascending or descending. Some views offer additional filter options for date ranges, categories, or status.

Export Options

Reports and data tables often include export functionality (CSV, Excel) for further analysis in external tools.

Quick Tips

  • Use the search bar in each list view to quickly find items.
  • Regularly check the low stock alerts on the dashboard.
  • Backup your data periodically (if using a database that supports exports).
  • Contact system administrator for access issues or feature requests.
  • Pay attention to notifications and alerts in the header.

Admin Role Overview

As an Administrator, you have full access to all system features. You are responsible for system configuration, user management, and overseeing all business operations.

Your dashboard includes comprehensive metrics covering all aspects of the business.

User Management

Managing User Accounts

Access via: Sidebar → User Management

As an admin, you can:

  • View all registered users in the system
  • Approve new user registrations (users must be approved before they can log in)
  • Assign roles to users (Admin, Manager, Cashier, Inventory Staff)
  • Activate/deactivate user accounts
  • Reset user passwords
  • Edit user profile information

To approve a user:

  1. Navigate to User Management
  2. Find users with "Pending Approval" status
  3. Select the user(s) you wish to approve
  4. Click the "Approve Selected" button
  5. Optionally assign a role if not already set

System Configuration

Settings Management

Access via: Sidebar → Settings (if available)

As admin, you may have access to:

  • Company information setup
  • Tax configuration
  • Currency settings
  • Notification preferences
  • Backup and restore options

Complete Access Privileges

As Administrator, you have access to ALL features in the system:

Inventory & Products
  • Categories management
  • Products management (create, edit, delete)
  • Suppliers management
  • Purchase orders management
  • Inventory adjustments and transfers
Sales & Customers
  • Point of Sale (POS) access
  • Customers management
  • Sales orders and invoices
  • Returns and refunds processing
  • Sales reports and analytics
Reports & Analytics
  • Sales reports
  • Inventory reports
  • Purchase reports
  • Financial reports
  • Audit logs and activity tracking
Administration
  • User management (as detailed above)
  • Role and permission configuration
  • System settings
  • Data import/export tools
  • Database maintenance

Admin Best Practices

  • Regularly review user access and permissions
  • Enable audit logging to track system changes
  • Schedule regular data backups
  • Keep the system updated with security patches
  • Train new users on role-appropriate workflows
  • Review reports regularly to identify business trends

Manager Role Overview

As a Manager, you have access to most business operations features with a focus on oversight, reporting, and team coordination. You cannot manage user accounts or system settings.

Your dashboard emphasizes sales performance, inventory levels, and key business metrics.

Sales and Customer Managementders Management

Managing Sales Processes

Access via: Sidebar → Products, Customers, Reports

As a manager, you can:

  • View and manage products (though deletion may be restricted)
  • View and manage customer information
  • Access the Point of Sale (POS) for sales processing
  • View sales history and generate sales reports
  • Manage customer groups or loyalty programs (if applicable)

Inventory Oversight

Monitoring Inventory Levels

Access via: Sidebar → Categories, Products, Suppliers, Purchase Orders

As a manager, you can:

  • View product categories and inventory levels
  • See low stock alerts and reorder recommendations
  • View and create purchase orders
  • Manage supplier information
  • Receive notifications for inventory issues
  • Generate inventory valuation reports

Reporting and Analytics

Accessing Business Intelligence

Access via: Sidebar → Reports

As a manager, you have access to:

  • Sales reports (daily, weekly, monthly, custom ranges)
  • Inventory reports (stock levels, turnover, valuation)
  • Purchase reports (order history, supplier performance)
  • Customer reports (purchase history, loyalty)
  • Financial summaries (revenue, expenses, profit margins)
  • Ability to filter reports by date, product, category, or customer
  • Export reports in various formats (PDF, CSV, Excel)

Manager Access Summary

As Manager, you have access to:

Available Features
  • Dashboard (business metrics view)
  • Products (view, edit - limited delete)
  • Categories (view, edit)
  • Suppliers (view, edit)
  • Purchase Orders (create, view, edit)
  • Customers (view, edit)
  • Point of Sale (full access)
  • Reports (all types)
Restricted Features
  • User Management
  • System Settings
  • Role assignments
  • Administrative tools
  • Audit log deletion

Note: Exact permissions may vary based on specific configuration.

Manager Best Practices

  • Review sales and inventory reports weekly
  • Monitor POS transactions for accuracy
  • Work with inventory staff to maintain optimal stock levels
  • Coordinate with cashiers on customer service issues
  • Review purchase orders and supplier performance
  • Gather feedback from team on system usability

Cashier Role Overview

As a Cashier, your primary focus is processing sales transactions and providing excellent customer service at the point of sale. You have limited access to inventory and customer information.

Your dashboard emphasizes quick access to sales functions and daily sales totals.

Point of Sale (POS) Operations

Processing Sales Transactions

Access via: Sidebar → POS

The POS interface is your primary workspace. Here you can:

  1. Search for products by name, SKU, or barcode
  2. Add products to the sale cart
  3. Apply discounts or promotions
  4. Process various payment methods (cash, card, etc.)
  5. Print or email receipts
  6. Handle returns and exchanges
  7. Manage customer lookup and creation

POS Workflow:

  1. Log in and navigate to POS
  2. Search for customer or create new customer record
  3. Scan/search for products and add to cart
  4. Apply any applicable discounts
  5. Select payment method
  6. Process payment and complete sale
  7. Provide receipt to customer

Customer Interactions

Managing Customer Information

Access via: Sidebar → Customers (limited) or POS customer lookup

As a cashier, you can:

  • Look up existing customers by name, phone, or email
  • Create new customer records during checkout
  • View basic customer information (contact details, purchase history)
  • Apply customer-specific discounts or loyalty benefits
  • Update customer contact information if needed

Limited Inventory Access

Checking Product Availability

Access via: POS search or limited Products view

As a cashier, you can:

  • Search for products to check availability
  • View basic product information (name, price, stock level)
  • See if items are in stock before adding to cart
  • Get notified if attempting to sell out-of-stock items
  • Suggest alternative products if preferred item is unavailable

Note: You cannot modify inventory levels or product details.

Cashier Access Summary

As Cashier, you have access to:

Available Features
  • Point of Sale (full access)
  • Customer lookup (view, create)
  • Product search (view only)
  • Basic sales reports (own performance)
  • Daily sales summary
Restricted Features
  • Product creation/editing/deletion
  • Inventory adjustments
  • Purchase orders
  • Supplier management
  • User management
  • Most reports (except personal sales)
  • System settings

Cashier Best Practices

  • Always verify product prices and stock before completing sale
  • Greet customers warmly and maintain professional demeanor
  • Double-check payment amounts and provide correct change
  • Keep your workspace clean and organized
  • Report any system issues or discrepancies to supervisor
  • Learn common product shortcuts for faster service

Inventory Staff Role Overview

As Inventory Staff, your primary responsibility is managing inventory levels, tracking stock movements, and ensuring accurate product information. You have access to inventory and purchasing functions but limited sales access.

Your dashboard emphasizes inventory metrics, low stock alerts, and pending purchase orders.

Inventory Management

Tracking and Managing Stock

Access via: Sidebar → Categories, Products

As inventory staff, you can:

  • Create, edit, and manage product categories
  • Create, edit, and manage products (including SKUs, descriptions, pricing)
  • Set reorder levels and stock thresholds
  • View real-time inventory levels and stock availability
  • Perform inventory adjustments (stock corrections, damage, loss)
  • Transfer inventory between locations (if applicable)
  • Run inventory counts and reconcile discrepancies

Purchase Order Management

Managing Supplier Procurement

Access via: Sidebar → Suppliers, Purchase Orders

As inventory staff, you can:

  • Create, edit, and manage supplier information
  • Create purchase orders based on reorder alerts or manual requests
  • View and track purchase order status (pending, received, partial)
  • Receive items against purchase orders
  • Generate purchase reports and supplier performance metrics
  • Manage supplier contacts and terms

Purchase Order Workflow:

  1. Identify low stock items via dashboard or reports
  2. Create purchase order with selected supplier
  3. Add required products and quantities
  4. Submit purchase order for approval
  5. Track delivery and receive items
  6. Update inventory levels upon receipt

Inventory Reporting

Analyzing Stock Performance

Access via: Sidebar → Reports

As inventory staff, you have access to:

  • Inventory levels reports (current stock, valuation)
  • Stock movement reports (ins, outs, adjustments)
  • Low stock and expiring items reports
  • Inventory turnover rates
  • ABC analysis (inventory categorization by value)
  • Purchase order reports (pending, received, vendor performance)
  • Ability to filter by category, supplier, date range

Inventory Access Summary

As Inventory Staff, you have access to:

Available Features
  • Products (full create, edit, view)
  • Categories (full create, edit, view)
  • Suppliers (full create, edit, view)
  • Purchase Orders (create, view, edit, receive)
  • Inventory reports and analytics
  • Limited product search in POS
Restricted Features
  • Point of Sale (view/search only)
  • Customer management (beyond basic lookup)
  • Sales order creation/modification
  • User management
  • System settings
  • Financial reports (revenue, profit)
  • Most sales reports

Inventory Best Practices

  • Conduct regular physical inventory counts
  • Keep product descriptions and SKUs accurate
  • Set appropriate reorder levels based on sales velocity
  • Monitor expiration dates for perishable items
  • Maintain good supplier relationships and communication
  • Document all inventory adjustments with reasons
